# Hotel Uniform and Dress Code Policy

## Uniform Requirements by Department

### Front Desk & Reception
- **Men**: White dress shirt, black trousers, hotel-provided blazer, black polished shoes, name badge
- **Women**: White blouse, black trousers or skirt (knee-length), hotel-provided blazer, black polished shoes, name badge
- Hair must be neatly styled; minimal jewelry allowed

### Housekeeping
- Hotel-provided uniform (color assigned by department)
- Comfortable, non-slip shoes (black or white)
- Apron and cap/hairnet (if required)
- Name badge on uniform

### Food & Beverage / Restaurants
- **Waitstaff**: Black dress pants, white shirt, black waistcoat, black shoes, black bow tie or scarf
- **Chefs/Kitchen**: White chef coat, white chef pants, black non-slip shoes, chef hat
- Hair must be tied back; no loose hair in kitchen area

### Maintenance & Operations
- Hotel-provided uniform or branded t-shirt
- Sturdy, closed-toe work shoes (safety requirement)
- Safety equipment as per role requirements
- Name badge

### Management & Administrative
- Business casual dress code
- Collared shirts, neat trousers/skirts, professional shoes
- Hotel branding items encouraged
- Name badge or identification required

## Uniform Maintenance

- Hotel provides 2-3 uniform sets per employee
- Uniforms must be cleaned and maintained in good condition
- Stains, tears, or damage must be reported immediately
- Replacement uniforms provided for worn-out items
- Employees are responsible for daily washing and ironing of uniforms
- Uniforms become property of the hotel; return upon resignation

## Grooming Standards

- **Cleanliness**: Daily bathing/shower is mandatory; maintain high personal hygiene
- **Hair**: Must be clean, neat, and well-groomed
  - For customer-facing roles: hair must be pinned back if longer than shoulder length
  - Kitchen/housekeeping: hair must always be tied back or covered
  - Visible hair colors must be natural tones (black, brown, blonde)
- **Facial Hair**: Must be clean-shaven or neatly trimmed beard
- **Nails**: Kept short and clean; nail polish only in natural colors for food handling staff
- **Perfume/Cologne**: Light application only; avoid strong fragrances
- **Tattoos**: Must be covered by uniform when possible; no offensive tattoos visible
- **Piercings**: Small, professional piercings only (nose studs, small earrings)

## Jewelry and Accessories

- Minimal jewelry allowed (1-2 small earrings, wedding band, watch)
- Bracelets, chains, or large rings prohibited for safety
- No religious symbols unless they comply with the dress code
- Manager approval required for exceptions

## Off-Duty Expectations

While off-duty, employees are ambassadors of the hotel:
- Maintain a professional appearance in hotel premises
- Avoid wearing extremely casual or revealing clothing
- If dining at the hotel, follow appropriate dress code

## Non-Compliance

- Failure to follow dress code results in verbal warning
- Repeated violations may result in suspension or termination
- Employees sent home for dress code violations will not be paid for that shift
