# Hotel Gratuity and Tips Policy

## Gratuity Acceptance

### Guest Tips
- Hotel guests may offer tips or gratuities for exceptional service
- Accepting tips is permitted and encouraged for excellent service
- Never refuse a guest's tip; graciously accept with a thank you

### Appropriate Acceptance
- Accept tips only from guests, not from other employees
- Do not expect or solicit tips from guests
- Small gifts from guests are acceptable (non-monetary items valued under $50)
- Politely decline gifts of significant value; inform manager if offered

## Tip Pooling and Distribution

### Gratuity Pooling
- All tips collected by the hotel (e.g., on bills) go into a shared pool
- This applies to front desk, housekeeping, and food service tips
- Pool is distributed fairly among relevant staff at month-end
- Distribution formula:
  - 40% to housekeeping and maintenance
  - 40% to food and beverage staff
  - 20% to front desk and administrative support

### Individual Tips
- Cash tips given directly to employees remain with the employee
- No pooling requirement for direct cash tips from guests
- However, team pooling is encouraged for fairness

## Tips and Taxes

- All tips are considered taxable income
- Tips are included in your gross income for tax purposes
- Tax deductions are made as per local labor laws
- Maintain records of tips received for tax compliance

## Reporting Tips

- Report all tips to HR for tax documentation
- Cash tips must be declared (even if not pooled)
- Missing tip declarations may result in penalties

## Non-Cash Gratuities

### Guest Gifts
- Small items (flowers, chocolates, souvenirs) may be accepted
- Valuable items or gift cards should be offered to management
- Gifts from guests must be reported to HR
- Do not accept gifts that could be perceived as bribes

## Prohibited Gratuity Practices

- Do not solicit tips from guests
- Do not demand or expect tips
- Do not refuse service based on inability to tip
- Do not discriminate against low-tipping guests
- Do not discuss tip amounts with guests

## Violation of Policy

- Employees found soliciting tips may face disciplinary action
- Theft of tips belonging to others is grounds for immediate termination
- Conflicts over tip distribution should be reported to HR
