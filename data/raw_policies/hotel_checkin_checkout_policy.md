# Hotel Check-In and Check-Out Policy

## Employee Check-In (Start of Shift)

### Arrival Requirements
- Arrive **15 minutes before your scheduled shift time**
- Enter through designated employee entrance
- Clock in using the time tracking system immediately upon arrival
- Ensure you are in uniform and ready to work 5 minutes before shift start

### Pre-Shift Checklist
- Collect your assignment details from the duty manager
- Check team communications board for updates
- Collect keys, access cards, or equipment needed for your role
- Report any maintenance issues or facility problems
- Greet and coordinate with the outgoing shift team (if applicable)

### Late Arrival Protocol
- Arriving more than 5 minutes late requires manager notification
- 3 late arrivals in a month may result in disciplinary action
- Repeated tardiness may affect shift allocation and performance review

## Employee Check-Out (End of Shift)

### End-of-Shift Responsibilities
- Complete all assigned tasks before shift end
- Return all keys, access cards, equipment, and uniforms
- Submit any incident reports or guest feedback
- Ensure your work area is clean and organized
- Clock out using the time tracking system

### Handover Procedure
- Brief the incoming shift team on any pending issues
- Provide report on guest requests or complaints
- Highlight any maintenance issues requiring attention
- Confirm all rooms/areas are secure

### Overtime and Extensions
- Overtime must be approved by your manager in advance
- Clock overtime separately from regular shift time
- Overtime compensation follows local labor regulations
- Employees cannot stay beyond 30 minutes after shift end without manager approval

## Attendance Tracking

- Digital time tracking system records all check-ins and check-outs
- Discrepancies must be reported to HR within 24 hours
- Consistent attendance is required for shift scheduling
- Perfect attendance is recognized in monthly performance reviews
