# Hotel Leave Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time hotel employees are entitled to:
- **Annual Leave**: 15-20 days per year, accrued monthly
- **Sick Leave**: 10 days per year for illness or medical appointments
- **Public Holidays**: Paid days off as per local regulations
- **Compassionate Leave**: Up to 3 days for family emergencies

### Leave Request Process
1. Submit leave request at least 14 days in advance (except emergencies)
2. Request through HR portal or speak to your department manager
3. Emergency leave must be communicated within 24 hours
4. Manager approval required; hotel management confirms based on operational needs

### Blackout Dates
Leave may be restricted during:
- Peak hotel seasons (holidays, major events)
- Festival seasons
- Convention periods in your city

Employees are requested to plan leave outside these periods.

## Shift Swaps and Time Off

- Employees may swap shifts with approval from management
- Manager must confirm both parties and operational coverage
- Shift swaps do not require HR approval if manager approves

## Leave Encashment

- Unused leave may be carried forward up to 5 days to the next year
- Remaining unused leave at year-end may be encashed as per local labor laws
- Upon resignation, all unused leave will be settled as per applicable regulations

## Extended Leave

- Sabbatical requests (unpaid) must be submitted in writing with 30 days notice
- Maximum 3 months unpaid leave per year, subject to business requirements
- Employee must confirm return date before sabbatical begins
