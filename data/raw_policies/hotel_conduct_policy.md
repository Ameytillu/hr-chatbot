# Hotel Staff Professional Conduct Policy

## Guest Service Excellence

### Service Standards
- Greet all guests with a warm, genuine welcome
- Use guests' names when possible and appropriate
- Respond to guest requests within 5 minutes (or as per SLA)
- Go the extra mile to ensure guest satisfaction
- Never refuse a reasonable guest request without manager approval

### Communication with Guests
- Speak clearly and professionally at all times
- Use "please," "thank you," and "my pleasure"
- Avoid using mobile phones while assisting guests
- Never argue with or be dismissive toward guests
- Escalate complaints to management immediately

## Workplace Conduct

### Professional Behavior
- Treat colleagues with respect and courtesy
- Maintain a positive, team-oriented attitude
- Report concerns through proper channels (not gossip)
- Avoid conflicts and resolve disputes professionally
- Support colleagues during busy periods

### Language and Communication
- Use professional language at all times (no slang, swearing, or offensive language)
- No gossiping about guests, colleagues, or management
- Keep confidential information private (guest details, staff issues)
- Use respectful tone in all communications

### Social Media and Technology
- Do not post about guests or internal hotel matters on social media
- Avoid posting negative comments about the hotel or colleagues
- Personal devices must not be used during work hours (except breaks)
- Hotel equipment (computers, phones) are for business use only
- Violations may result in disciplinary action

## Health and Safety

### Safety Responsibility
- Follow all safety procedures and protocols
- Report hazards, accidents, or injuries immediately
- Use safety equipment as required for your role
- Never ignore safety warnings or procedures
- Participate in mandatory safety training

### Hygiene Standards
- Maintain strict personal hygiene at all times
- Wash hands before food handling and after restroom breaks
- Cover cuts or wounds while working
- Do not work if ill (report to manager and stay home)
- Follow cleaning and sanitization protocols

## Workplace Respect and Inclusion

### Zero Tolerance Policy
- No discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or disability
- No harassment of any kind (verbal, physical, or sexual)
- No bullying or intimidation
- Violations are grounds for immediate disciplinary action

### Respectful Workplace
- Address colleagues by their preferred names
- Respect personal space and boundaries
- Support an inclusive environment for all team members
- Report harassment or discrimination to HR immediately

## Performance and Accountability

### Punctuality and Attendance
- Attend all scheduled shifts on time
- Notify manager immediately if you cannot work
- Excessive absenteeism may result in disciplinary action

### Quality of Work
- Complete tasks to hotel standards
- Take pride in your work; double-check for errors
- Accept feedback and strive for continuous improvement
- Request help if unsure how to complete a task

### Accountability
- Take responsibility for your actions and mistakes
- Don't blame others for your errors
- Proactively solve problems or escalate appropriately
- Learn from feedback and adapt your behavior

## Disciplinary Actions

Non-compliance with this policy may result in:
1. **Verbal Warning**: First offense (documented)
2. **Written Warning**: Second offense (placed in file)
3. **Suspension**: Serious or repeated violations
4. **Termination**: Gross misconduct or continuous violations

Gross misconduct (theft, violence, harassment, etc.) may result in immediate termination.
